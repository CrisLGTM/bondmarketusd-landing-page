What are fractionalized bonds?+

A corporate bond is money a company borrows from investors. Fractionalized bonds divide that debt into smaller, tradeable token units. Your rights depend on the product terms.

Does 24/7 include weekends?+

Yes, including weekends. Around-the-clock access does not guarantee that an order will fill at your chosen price.

What do I need, and how small can I start?+

Fund with USDC and start below $1. Consider any applicable funding, network and trading costs separately from your position size.

Do bonds pay interest?+

A coupon is a bond’s stated interest payment. Eligible holdings receive a proportional share on the issuer’s schedule, subject to the product terms and the issuer meeting its obligations.

Can I go long, go short, or lend?+

You can go long (take a view that prices will rise) or short (take a view that they will fall), with optional leverage to increase exposure. You can also lend supported assets under separate terms. Lending differs from receiving a bond’s coupon.

What should I know about the risks?+

Prices can fall, issuers can default and you can lose money. Leverage magnifies losses and adds borrowing costs and liquidation risk—the risk of your position being closed. Lending also carries risk; returns are not guaranteed.
