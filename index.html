New to bonds? Here’s the short version +

A bond is a loan to a company. Its coupon describes the stated interest payment; maturity is when repayment is due under its terms. Fractionalization makes smaller units available to trade.

Prices can rise or fall, and issuers can default. Your rights and payments depend on the product’s terms.
